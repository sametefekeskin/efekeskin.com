Strategic Segmentation & Machine Learning for the UK Housing Market

Data science project with Arda Kırayoğlu.
We analyzed over 180,000 property transactions from the UK Land Registry (2015–2025) to evaluate real estate investment strategies for a private equity client. The project combined K-Means Clustering for market segmentation and Random Forest Regression for intrinsic value modeling. Key findings emphasized that location explains over 70% of price variation, supporting a geographically targeted investment strategy.
